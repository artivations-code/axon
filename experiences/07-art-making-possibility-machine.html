The Art-Making Possibility Machine

Spin four reels, lock what you like, and get a specific invitation to make something with real materials.

Press Spin all, then Compose invitation. Lock any reel you want to keep.

How this page works

Spin all four reels, or spin one. Lock a reel to keep it while the others move. Step moves one reel forward one item, so you can choose instead of chance.

Compose turns the combination into an invitation. Compose again for a different wording of the same four.

Save what you want to keep to the shelf. Add your own words to any reel.

Everything stays on this device. Start fresh clears it.

These are invitations for making. They are not interpretations, and the machine does not know you. Refuse any part, swap a word, or set the whole thing down. The materials are ordinary. Most are already in a kitchen drawer.
