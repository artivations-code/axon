The Cabinet of Unfinished Things

Open the compartments, set two found objects on the worktable, and read what the cabinet tells, which changes with what you leave open.

Click a compartment, or tab to it and press Enter. Open ones stay open until you close them.

How this page works

Each open compartment shows one fragment and gives you one found object. Close it and the object goes back in.

Put two objects on the worktable. Some pairs have a connection written. For the rest, you can write your own, and it is kept on this device.

The drawer marked Yours takes your own unfinished things: a title, some writing, an image if you like. Each becomes a found object too.

The panel called What the cabinet is telling changes with what is open and what has been joined. Leave it unfinished closes everything and keeps your own writing. Start fresh clears all of it.
