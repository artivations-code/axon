01Your path into the field

How you become an art therapist.

Art therapy is a distinct mental-health profession. Here’s the road from student to credentialed practitioner — and where Indiana is working to make it official.

01

Explore the field

Learn what art therapists actually do across hospitals, schools, clinics, and communities. Talk to practitioners and sit in on IndiATA events.

02

Earn a master’s degree

Complete a graduate program in art therapy (or a related field with the required coursework) accredited by the field’s standards.

03

Gain supervised experience

Build clinical hours under a qualified supervisor — the practice that turns coursework into real-world skill.

04

Earn your credential (ATR)

Apply for the Registered Art Therapist credential (and beyond) through the national credentials board to formally enter the profession.

05

Advocate in Indiana

Indiana doesn’t yet license art therapists. As a student, your voice helps win title protection and access to care statewide.
