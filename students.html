03 — Considering the Student Representative role 4 questions
What does a Student Representative actually do?

Representatives share accurate IndiATA information, gather student questions and ideas, join brief cross-program check-ins, contribute to one bounded initiative at a time, and leave a usable handoff for the next representative.

How much time does the role take?

Plan for an average of two to four hours per month during active semesters. Finals, internship demands, work, health, caregiving, and other capacity changes can be named and planned around.

Does a Student Representative vote or sit on the Executive Board?

No. This is an appointed, nonvoting volunteer liaison role. Representatives do not count toward quorum, hold an officer position, or receive unrestricted confidential Board material.

What support and recognition are included?

Representatives have a Board Liaison, shared project leadership, clear task boundaries, access planning, and a named route for concerns. Completed service may be documented through a confirmation letter and a CV-ready role description.

04 — Joining an IndiATA committee 2 questions
How do I join a committee?

Pick the committee in the Share form — Multicultural, Continuing Education, or Advocacy (or “Help me find a committee”) — and note what you’d like to contribute and the time you realistically have. The committee chair follows up about current priorities and whether an ongoing role or one bounded task fits.

Express committee interest
Can I be a Student Representative and join a committee?

Possibly. The roles are separate. A Student Representative appointment does not place you on a committee, and committee participation does not create a representative appointment. Name your capacity before taking both so each role stays clear and limited.

Ask about both roles
05 — Membership and applying 3 questions
I want to become a Student Representative, but my AATA or IndiATA student membership is not active.

The representative interest form is for current student members. Establish active AATA student membership and add IndiATA through MyAATA before submitting it. You may contact IndiATA with membership questions before deciding whether to move forward.

Learn about membership
Do I need faculty approval, a GPA, portfolio, recommendation, résumé, or photograph?

No. Faculty may share the opportunity, but they do not appoint representatives. The interest process does not request a GPA, transcript, portfolio, recommendation letter, résumé, photograph, disability documentation, diagnosis disclosure, or personal trauma narrative.

Does submitting the form mean I am appointed?

No. The form begins the process. A designated reviewer follows up for a brief conversation, and the Executive Board appoints representatives by recorded motion.

06 — Program, transition, and access situations 2 questions
I need another communication format or access support.

Use the access field on a form or email IndiATA. You may ask for an asynchronous conversation, another way to respond, or communication limited to the information needed for the question at hand — use the Contact Us page in the site menu.

I have a concern about the representative process or an interaction.

Contact the Student Engagement Liaison or IndiATA directly. Share the specific concern, the response you are seeking, and whether you want your identity kept limited to the people needed to respond.
