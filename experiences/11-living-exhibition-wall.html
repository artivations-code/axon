The Living Exhibition Wall

Pan a salon-style wall of IndiATA exhibition work, follow the threads between pieces, and open any work to read its label, statement, and process.

How to use this
  • Drag the wall, scroll sideways, or use the arrow buttons. The left and right keys move between works.
  • Choose a theme to draw a thread between the works that share it. Choose more than one and each thread keeps its own line.
  • Open a work to read its full label, the artist statement, and the process steps.
  • Switch between the two exhibitions above the wall.
  • Curator mode adds, edits, reorders, and removes works. Export JSON to keep a copy outside this browser.

Artwork and statements appear as the artists supplied them. The page does not edit, crop, or interpret them.
