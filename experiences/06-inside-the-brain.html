Inside the Brain

Touch four parts of the brain to find out what each one does, where you can feel it in your body, and one art activity to try.

How to use this
  • Touch a part of the brain, or its name under the picture, to open it.
  • Each part has a picture, a thing to feel in your body, and an art activity.
  • Switch between For kids and For grown-ups any time. Both are true. One uses more words.
  • Press Where is it in me? to light up the place on the body.
  • Parts you have opened get a green check. Nothing is scored.
