About our role

Seeking art therapy? Use our directory — we are not a service provider. IndiATA does not provide therapy or match people with individual therapists. We are Indiana's professional association for art therapists.

Understanding the Profession

What Is Art Therapy?

Art therapy is a distinct, master's-level mental health profession. It integrates artmaking and creative processes with psychological theory, clinical assessment, and psychotherapy within a therapeutic relationship.

Clinical Practice

Art therapists use specialized graduate education and supervised clinical training to assess needs, establish treatment goals, and work collaboratively with clients through artmaking, images, and meaning-making.

Using art materials within another profession or activity does not, by itself, constitute art therapy.

National art therapy credentials: ATR-P, ATR, ATR-BC, and ATCS are issued by the Art Therapy Credentials Board (ATCB). Verify current ATCB credentials.
