How To Use The Book At Home

Use short language. Keep meanings spacious. Medical concerns stay medical.

Rigidity

“Sounds like the alarm found one road. Let’s look for two more.”

Food

“Bodies use many supplies. We do not have to argue with the body.”

Hands

“I see your hands looking for a job. Want putty, fabric, or stone?”

Separation

“Close has steps. Which step are we on?”
