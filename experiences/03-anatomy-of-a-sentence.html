The Anatomy of a Sentence

Take one sentence apart to see what it assumes, leaves out, permits, and implies, then swap single words and watch the meaning move.

How to use this
  1. Pick an example from the shelf. The original stays at the top the whole time.
  2. Turn on a layer to see which words carry it. Hover or tap a note to find its words.
  3. Try a word toggle. A revision line builds underneath. Keep it or discard it.
  4. Paste your own sentence at the bottom and annotate it yourself. Your work saves in this browser.
