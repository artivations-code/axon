01Student community

Ways to get involved — start where you are.

Connect with students across all three Indiana programs, and choose the level of involvement that fits your semester. No single path is required.

01 / Ongoing

Student representative

An appointed, nonvoting student liaison between students in your program and IndiATA. Up to two representatives may be appointed from each program.

~2–4 hrs / month
02 / Project

Committee member

Join work that matches your interests — Multicultural, Continuing Education, or Advocacy. Project-based; you’re not on the hook for every chapter task.

As much or as little as you choose
03 / One-time

One bounded contribution

Help with a single thing — an event, a resource, a newsletter section, a Student Pulse question, or a cross-program gathering. A title isn’t required.

No ongoing commitment

Interested in any of these? Email the chapter at Indianaarttherapy@gmail.com — or use the form below.
