The Inner World Atlas

Draw a map of a place that is yours. Name its regions, move them, link them, cover some in fog, and come back to it another day.

How to use this
  1. Click a region to name it, write a note, pick a symbol or a color, or cover it with fog.
  2. Drag a region to move it. When a region has focus, the arrow keys move it too.
  3. Press Connect, then click two regions to draw a path between them. Give the path a word.
  4. Everything saves on this device. Download the file to carry the atlas somewhere else.
  5. To make a paper version, 140 lb cold-press watercolor paper takes pencil, ink, and wash without buckling.
