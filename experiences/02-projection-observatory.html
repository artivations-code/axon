A concept from analytical psychology

Projection

In analytical psychology, projection names the act of attributing contents of one's own psyche to an outside object. The object is then experienced as holding what the person has not yet recognized as their own. Jung held that a projection needs a hook, some real feature of the object for it to hang on. The marked words in Layer 1 are the hook.

Art therapists know the word from projective drawing tasks like House-Tree-Person. This page follows a different rule. The person who made the image says what it means.

The concept describes a human mind. It does not decide what an AI system is, and neither does this page. The page shows how an interpretation moves while the evidence stays still.
