The Adoption Constellation

Place the parts of your adoption story as stars, and set them as near or far from each other as they are for you.

Drag a star to place it. Click a star to name it.

How this page works

Drag a star to move it, or tab to a star and press the arrow keys. In Move mode, clicking a star lets you rename it, write a private note, dim it, or remove it.

To draw a line, choose a line type, switch to Link, then click two stars. The same two stars can hold more than one line.

Add an undefined area for anything you are choosing not to name yet.

The starting places mean nothing. Nothing here is scored, sorted, or read back to you.

Nothing here needs to resolve. Two stars can be close and in tension at the same time. A star can stay blank. Space can stay undefined.

This page does not suggest an arrangement, and it never comments on where you put things. What you make stays in this browser on this device. No one else sees it unless you export it and choose to share it. This page is not therapy.
