Student community

Ways to get involved.

Connect with students across all three Indiana programs, and choose the level of involvement that fits your semester. No single path is required — start where you are.

Student representative

Serve as an appointed, nonvoting student liaison between students in your program and IndiATA. Up to two representatives may be appointed from each program.

~2–4 hrs/month · works around classes, internship, work, and life

Committee member

Join work that matches your interests — Multicultural, Continuing Education, or Advocacy. Project-based; you’re not on the hook for every chapter task.

Project-based · as much or as little as you choose

One bounded contribution

Help with a single thing — an event, a resource, a newsletter section, a Student Pulse question, or a cross-program gathering. A title isn’t required.

One-time · no ongoing commitment

Interested in any of these? Email the chapter at Indianaarttherapy@gmail.com — or use the form at the bottom of this page.
