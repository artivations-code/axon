About Beyond the Sun

An adoption-constellation practice with lived experience on the team, clinical depth in the work, and no agency ties.

Beyond the Sun is a private therapy and coaching practice in Carmel, Indiana. We were built specifically for people whose lives have been shaped by adoption, separation, family-building complexity, and the grief and relational weight that come with those experiences.

What makes the practice different is not a modality list — it is the combination of lived experience, clinical training, and a refusal to flatten adoption into something simpler than it is.

The team includes an adoptee art psychotherapist with specific training in nervous-system and identity work, and a clinician with direct birth family experience. We do not offer adoption support as an add-on specialty. It is the center of what we do.

We are independent of every agency, placement organization, and adoption program in Indiana. Our clients are not agency clients. They are people at any point in the adoption story who need real, ongoing, constellation-aware support — regardless of when or how their connection to adoption began.

Beyond the Sun offers individual counseling, coaching, art psychotherapy, and neuro-expressive approaches for individuals, families, and the outer circle. We work with children, teens, adults, and people across the lifespan. Virtual and in-person sessions available in Indiana.
