Beyond The Sun Therapy

Twelve rooms to explore, move, question, and change

Interactive work built around art, writing, art psychotherapy, adoption, and IndiATA. In each room, interaction changes what can be examined.

Each page runs on its own, keeps what you make on this device, and exports it as text or JSON. Draft and example content is labeled as such.

Art, surrealism, and writing

Creative La'boratory

  1. The Cabinet of Unfinished Things

    Surreal narrative

    Drawers and doors open into fragments, creatures, and unfinished images. Two found objects on the worktable change how the story unfolds. One compartment stays empty on purpose.

    Open the cabinet

  2. The Projection Observatory

    AI and projection

    One exchange with an AI, three layers. The record stays still while the attribution lens moves what is read into it and what remains unknown.

    Change the lens

  3. The Anatomy of a Sentence

    Literary instrument

    A sentence's assumptions, omissions, ambiguities, and implications, revealed in place. Swap one word and see precisely what shifts. The original stays intact.

    Examine a sentence

Art psychotherapy, adoption, and identity

Beyond The Sun Therapy

  1. The Inner World Atlas

    Illustrated map

    A chart of a fictional inner world. Rename regions, move them, connect them, cover them in fog, and return over many sessions. Nothing assigns a meaning.

    Open the atlas

  2. The Adoption Constellation

    Non-linear field

    Identity, relationships, belonging, loss, anger, joy, and unanswered questions in one private sky. Move them, link them, let them contradict, leave space undefined.

    Place the stars

  3. Inside the Brain

    For children and adults

    Touch the amygdala, hippocampus, prefrontal cortex, or insula. Each opens an explanation, a sensory example, and an art activity, in a child's words or an adult's.

    Touch a region

  4. The Art-Making Possibility Machine

    Invitation generator

    A material, a sensory quality, an artistic operation, and an expressive constraint compose a materially specific invitation. Never an interpretation.

    Spin the reels

IndiATA, professional advocacy, and public education

Indiana Art Therapy Association

  1. The Art Therapy Boundary Lab

    Branching scenarios

    Schools, libraries, hospitals, community programs. Decide on facilitation, scope, accessibility, boundaries, and response to distress, and see what each choice commits a program to.

    Choose a setting

  2. Indiana Art Therapy: The Living Map

    Documented landscape

    Practitioners, service access, telehealth, educational pathways, and workforce gaps by region. Documented entries are solid. Everything else says so.

    Open the map

  3. Build an Art Therapy Program

    Workspace for employers

    Choose setting, population, services, staffing, supervision, accessibility, and resources. A program structure, staffing plan, job description, and budget framework assemble as you choose.

    Begin a draft

  4. The Living Exhibition Wall

    Online exhibition

    A salon-style wall for Art Therapists of the Midwest or Trust the Process. Follow thematic threads, open statements and process documentation. Artwork stays as the artist supplied it.

    Walk the wall

  5. The Advocacy Consequences Explorer

    Branching model

    Licensure, hiring qualifications, title representation, workforce access. The people, institutions, requirements, and decisions each touches, with enacted, proposed, and hypothetical kept distinct.

    Pick a proposal

No page interprets a person's imagery, diagnoses, or stands in for therapy. Pages that touch distress say so and name 988.
