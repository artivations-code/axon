Adoption is a lifelong unfolding.

Adoption-Attuned Therapy, Coaching & Creative Care in Carmel, Indiana

For adoptees, birth and first parents, adoptive families, and everyone connected to the constellation — across the full lifespan, at any stage.

Adoption shapes lives in ways that do not stay neatly inside a chapter. The nervous system remembers early separation even when the mind cannot. Identity questions arrive uninvited. Relationships carry weight that is hard to name. Grief does not follow a timeline.

Beyond the Sun is an adoption-attuned therapy and creative care practice in Carmel, Indiana. We serve the full adoption constellation: adoptees of all ages, birth and first parents, adoptive parents, late-discovery adults, and the extended family and relational networks that carry adoption impact too.

We offer counseling, coaching, art psychotherapy, and neuro-expressive work — independent of any agency, placement process, or program. This is not a post-placement checkbox. It is ongoing, whole-person support for the real and layered life of adoption, at whatever point in the story you are living.

The Constellation, Not Just the Triad

We work with adoptees, birth mothers, birth fathers, adoptive parents, late-discovery adults, and outer-circle family members who are rarely named and rarely served. Adoption belongs to no single role.

Nervous System First

The body carries what words have not yet reached. Our work begins with how adoption lives in the nervous system — not as a metaphor, but as a clinical framework that changes what is possible in healing.

Therapy and Beyond

Some people need clinical therapy. Others need coaching, creative process, or a combination that does not fit a standard intake form. We offer all of it, and we help you figure out what fits.
