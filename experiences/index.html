What these pages are, and are not

  • Each page runs on its own, saves to the device it runs on, and exports what the person made as text or JSON.
  • Example content inside is labeled as example or draft. Facts that need a source are labeled "Needs source confirmation".
  • No page interprets a person's imagery, diagnoses, or stands in for therapy. Pages that touch distress say so and point to 988.
